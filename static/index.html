6-DOF Robotic Arm Control Center Operating Manual & User Guide

Complete Setup, 3D Kinematics Pose Control, Servo Trim Calibration, Gemini AI Voice & Vision, and Maintenance Instructions

Step 1

⚡ Quick Start & System Power On

  • 1. Power Supply Connection: Connect a 2S (7.4V) or 3S (11.1V) LiPo battery to the high-current power terminal bus. Ensure the 5V 10A UBEC powers PCA9685 servos and Pi logic.
  • 2. Start Web Controller: Launch the Python server terminal command:
    python3 server.py
  • 3. Open Workbench: Navigate to http://localhost:1090 in your web browser.
  • 4. Connection Status: Ensure the status badge at top right displays ONLINE.
Step 2

🎮 3D Kinematics & Joint Control

  • • Joint Angle Sliders: Individually adjust Base Rotation J1 (-90° to +90°), Shoulder J2 (-90° to +90°), Elbow J3 (-135° to +135°), Wrist Tilt J4 (-90° to +90°), Wrist Roll J5 (-90° to +90°), and Gripper Claw J6 (0% to 100%).
  • • Realtime Inverse Kinematics (IK): Inspect 3D End-Effector target coordinates (X, Y, Z in mm) updated live in the left panel.
  • • On-Screen Motion D-Pad: Use ▲/▼ for Shoulder pitch, ◄/► for Base rotation, and 👐/✊ for Claw opening & closing.
Step 3

🔧 6-Servo Trim Calibration Workbench

  • 1. Calibration Tab: Switch to 🔧 Servo Calibration view to inspect the live 3D preview and fine-tune trim offsets.
  • 2. Joint Trim Adjustments: Calibrate individual offset angles (-30° to +30°) for all 6 servos to eliminate mechanical assembly error.
  • 3. Quick Arm Presets: Test hardware alignment using quick buttons: Orchestra Conductor 🎼, Wave Arm Dance 🕺, Yes (Nod) 👍, No (Shake) 👎, High Five ✋, Home / Rest 🏠, and Pick Object 📦.
Step 4

🤖 Gemini AI Multimodal Assistant & Camera Vision

  • 1. API Key Setup: Enter your Google Gemini API key in the AI panel or under ⚙️ Settings & HW.
  • 2. Activate Microphone: Click 🎙️ Activate Mic for live hands-free speech recognition with VU meter sensitivity gain control.
  • 3. Camera Scene Description: Click 👁️ Describe Scene to capture eMeet Pixy webcam frames and have Gemini speak environment descriptions out loud!
Step 5

🎵 MP3 Music Conductor & Lip-Sync Studio

  • 1. Import Music Files: Click 📁 Import MP3 to static/audio/ Folder to load songs into local storage.
  • 2. Arm Conductor Styles: Choose between Orchestra Conductor, Symphony Maestro Sweep, Rock Concert Conductor, Wave Arm Dance, and Arm Disco Dance.
  • 3. Playback & Lip-Sync: Click ▶️ Conduct & Lip-Sync. The mouth DC motor automatically opens during speech/song vocals!
Step 6

📡 Autonomous Rangefinding & Maintenance

  • • Ultrasonic Rangefinder: HC-SR04 sensor continuously monitors front obstacle distances in real time.
  • • Distance Alert Safeguard: Toggle Enable Proximity Distance Warning in Settings to prevent arm collisions.
  • • Battery Protection: Disconnect LiPo battery when not in use. Verify 1kΩ / 2kΩ resistor dividers step down 5V Echo signals to 3.3V Pi GPIO levels.
